Our Story

Helping Businesses Access Genuine Mobile Devices

Universal telecom was established with a clear goal—to make wholesale smartphone purchasing simple, reliable and affordable. We understand the challenges retailers and distributors face when sourcing quality devices, so we built a business focused on authenticity, competitive pricing and dependable customer service.

Today, Universal telecom continues to grow by building lasting relationships with retailers, corporate organizations and entrepreneurs, providing them with quality products backed by professional support.
